# Decision to release 0.4.1

Release all four packages at `0.4.1` through protected CI, with the companions peering on `~0.4.1`, after the stable gates pass. Carry the open P1s, the benchmark evidence and the Task 8 findings at the dispositions recorded in the [readiness review](../plans/evidence/042-release-readiness-review.md).

## Approval

The owner answered the two review questions on 2026-11-24:

> Deferrals: "Approve all (Recommended)"

> Release: "Go"

| Item | Disposition |

| Backlog P1 "Fix initially-open dialogs in the demo host" | Carried, as at 0.4.0. The registry demo passed 78 of 78 against `0.4.1-rc.2`; 0.4.1 changed no renderer or demo code. |

## Alternatives and rationale

1. Fix A4–A6 before the release. They are patch-safe, but an agent repaired both errors without them, and the release carries security fixes adopters are waiting for.
2. Hold for a quiet-host benchmark pass. The host cannot provide one, and plan 049 owns making the rule passable. LF-18's and LF-34's new gates held under load.
